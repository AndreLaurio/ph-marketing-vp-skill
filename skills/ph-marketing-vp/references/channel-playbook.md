# Channel playbook (PH)

Each channel: when to use it, the play, rough cost, the metric that matters. Costs are
directional ranges for planning, not quotes. Ad prices move with season (Q4 is the
most expensive), so tell the user to test small before scaling.

## Facebook Page + Messenger (almost always: the home base)

- **Use when**: always, for B2C. It is the storefront people check before buying.
- **Play**: complete page (cover with offer, price list, location, hours, GCash
  details in a pinned post), Messenger saved replies and an instant greeting with
  price list + "how to order", 3-5 posts/week mixing feedback, product, behind the
  scenes, and one offer. Reels from phone footage get reach that photos no longer do.
- **Paid**: boost only posts that already performed organically. Start ₱100-300/day
  for 3-5 days on a "Messages" objective, local radius targeting. Click-to-Messenger
  ads fit how Filipinos buy. Install Meta Pixel on the website before any website
  traffic ads.
- **Metric**: inquiries (new Messenger conversations) per week, cost per
  conversation, reply time.

## Facebook Groups (usually the first organic channel)

See `facebook-groups-playbook.md`. Metric: DMs and orders attributed per group.

## Facebook Marketplace

- **Use when**: physical products, furniture, pre-loved, local services, real
  estate rentals, cars. Free, local-first.
- **Play**: list each product separately with real photos and price; renew listings;
  answer "Is this still available?" within minutes.

## TikTok (+ TikTok Shop)

- **Use when**: visual products, food, beauty, fashion, gadgets, home finds,
  anything with a demo or transformation. Strong for Gen Z and younger millennials,
  increasingly mass.
- **Play**: 1-2 videos/day for 30 days beats 3 polished videos. Formats: "POV",
  unboxing, process, before/after, owner's story, answering comments with video.
  Raw phone footage, trending sounds, Taglish captions, on-screen text.
- **TikTok Shop**: affiliate program lets creators sell your product for a
  commission; seed free samples to 20-50 nano creators. Live selling works for
  repeat-purchase products.
- **Metric**: views per video, saves/shares, profile visits, orders.

## Instagram

- **Use when**: ABC1 audience, aesthetics-driven (cafes, fashion, weddings, interiors,
  wellness). Secondary to FB for most mass brands.
- **Play**: Reels cross-posted from TikTok, highlight covers for menu/price/how to
  order/feedback, Close Friends for VIP drops.

## Shopee / Lazada / TikTok Shop marketplaces

- **Use when**: shippable products nationwide, competing on price/convenience.
- **Play**: optimize titles with search terms buyers use, 9 real photos + video,
  collect reviews early (follow-up chat, small freebie in parcel with "rate us"
  card), join campaign days (see calendar), vouchers, free-shipping programs.
  Marketplace ads (Shopee Ads, Sponsored Solutions) once listings convert.
- **Watch**: commission and fees cut margin; price wars. Use marketplace for
  reach and your own channels (Messenger, Viber community) for repeat buyers.
- **Metric**: conversion rate per listing, rating, repeat purchase.

## Google Business Profile (Google Maps) + Search

- **Use when**: any business with a location or service area: clinics, food,
  salons, repair, schools, hotels, B2B suppliers. "Near me" searches matter in cities.
- **Play**: verify listing, correct category, hours, photos weekly, ask every
  happy customer for a Google review (QR on counter/receipt), reply to all reviews.
  Simple website or landing page with clear phone/Messenger CTA.
- **Paid**: Google Search ads for high-intent services ("dentist near Ortigas",
  "aircon cleaning Quezon City"); start ₱300-1,000/day with tight keywords.
- **Metric**: calls, direction requests, review count and rating.

## Viber Communities / Telegram

- **Use when**: repeat purchase (food, groceries, pet supplies), VIP lists, B2B
  distributors. Viber is popular among older and professional users; Telegram
  among some tech/crypto/gaming audiences.
- **Play**: invite buyers after purchase; post menu/drops, early access, restock
  alerts. Not more than 1-2 posts/day.

## Influencers / KOLs

- **Tiers**: nano (1k-10k), micro (10k-100k), macro and celebrities. Small
  businesses should start with nano/micro local creators: cheaper, more trusted,
  often accept product + modest fee. Rates vary widely; ask for their rate card and
  past results (screenshots of reach, not just followers).
- **Play**: brief with one message and one offer, let them speak in their own
  voice, give a unique code to measure. Prefer repeat partnerships over one-offs.
- **Rules**: sponsored content should be disclosed (#ad / paid partnership);
  health/beauty/food claims still fall under FDA rules.

## Referrals and resellers

- **Referral**: "Refer a friend, both get ₱50 off" or free item. Filipinos already
  recommend; give them a reason and a script.
- **Resellers / distributors**: classic PH growth engine for products (food,
  beauty, wellness): tiered pricing, starter kits, a resellers' group chat, ready
  photos and captions. Recruit in reseller/negosyo groups. Avoid anything
  structured like a pyramid; reward sales, not recruitment.

## Offline (still a channel)

- Tarpaulin at the store or on the gate (cheap, local), flyers at subdivisions with
  permission, sampling at bazaars/tiangge/weekend markets, barangay or parish events,
  school fairs, mall kiosks (pop-ups), partnerships with neighboring businesses
  (cafe + bakery cross-promo), jeepney/tricycle ads in provinces, local radio in
  provinces. Always add a QR to the FB page or Messenger to measure.

## B2B channels

- LinkedIn (decision makers in Manila/Cebu corporate), industry Facebook groups,
  chambers of commerce (PCCI and local chambers), trade fairs (e.g. at SMX/World
  Trade Center), associations, PhilGEPS for government procurement, direct email and
  call outreach to a built list (pair with the `lead-scraper` skill), case studies,
  free trials/demos, and relationship selling: lunch meetings still close deals.

## Email / SMS

- Email works for B2B and ABC1 ecommerce; less for mass B2C.
- SMS blasts require consent; treat phone numbers as personal data (Data Privacy
  Act). Never buy number lists.

## Default budget splits (adjust to the archetype)

| Monthly budget | Split |
|---|---|
| ₱0 | 100% time: groups, page, Google Maps, referrals, partnerships |
| ₱3,000-10,000 | 60% boosts on proven posts / click-to-Messenger, 25% samples or nano creators, 15% tools/props (ring light, Canva Pro, CapCut Pro) |
| ₱10,000-50,000 | 50% paid social, 20% creators, 15% Google Search or marketplace ads, 15% content production |
| ₱50,000+ | add a part-time content creator/VA, structured creator program, retargeting with Pixel, testing new channels with 10-15% |
