# Facebook groups playbook (PH)

Groups are where Filipino buyers ask for recommendations, compare, and trust each
other. For most small PH businesses they are the cheapest first channel. They are
also the easiest place to get kicked out by acting like an advertiser.

The skill cannot search inside Facebook and must not scrape it. Give the user the
search terms, the vetting criteria and the plan; they do the joining.

## 1. Find the right groups (give the user exact search terms)

Search in the Facebook app, filter by "Groups". Build terms from these patterns:

| Group type | Search terms (swap in city/niche) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Local community / updates | "<City> Updates", "Taga <Town>", "<Subdivision> Residents", "<Condo> Owners and Tenants" | any local B2C, services, food |
| Buy & sell (local) | "<City> Buy and Sell", "<Province> Online Selling", "BnS <City>" | products, food, pre-loved |
| Niche interest | "<City> Foodies", "Mommies of <City>", "<Breed> Owners PH", "Runners PH", "Plantitas PH" | anything matching the interest |
| Problem / life stage | "First Time Moms PH", "WFH Pinoys", "Newly Weds PH", "Condo Living PH" | services, baby, home, courses |
| Recommendation groups | "<City> Recommendations", "Saan maganda <niche>", "Legit sellers <city>" | trust-heavy services |
| Professional / B2B | "SME Owners PH", "HR Practitioners PH", "Online Sellers PH", "Restaurant Owners PH" | B2B, tools, suppliers |
| Reseller / dropship | "Resellers PH", "Dropshipping PH", "Negosyo Ideas PH" | recruiting resellers/distributors |
| OFW | "OFW <Country>", "Pinoys in <City abroad>" | padala, gifts, property, remittance-adjacent |

Also WebSearch `site:facebook.com/groups "<niche>" "<city>"` to surface public groups,
and ask existing customers "anong groups kayo active?" — the best list comes from them.

## 2. Vet before joining (5 minutes per group)

Score each group; join the top 5-10:

- **Activity**: posts per day and comments per post (visible in "About" and feed).
  A 200k-member group with 3 comments per post is dead; a 12k local group with 40
  comments per post is gold.
- **Rules**: selling allowed? Only on certain days ("Selling Saturday")? Only in
  comments or a pinned thread? Links banned? Screenshot the rules.
- **Audience fit**: are the posters your buyer (location, life stage, budget)?
- **Admin behavior**: do admins remove spam (good) or is it a wall of ads (useless)?
- **Competitor presence**: competitors there with engagement = proven demand.

Record in a simple tracker: group, link, members, posts/day, selling rules, best day,
admin name, status.

## 3. Behave like a member first (the 80/20 rule)

- Join from the owner's **personal profile** (groups often restrict Page posting;
  a real face builds trust). Complete the profile: real photo, work at <business>.
- Week 1-2: no selling. Answer questions in your expertise, react, welcome people.
  10-15 helpful comments per group per week.
- Roughly 4 value contributions to every 1 promotional post. Value = tips,
  answering "saan maganda...?" honestly (even recommending others sometimes),
  behind-the-scenes, local news relevant to the niche.
- When someone asks for a recommendation that you fit, reply helpfully and let
  happy customers tag you. Customers tagging you beats you tagging yourself.

## 4. Post types that sell without looking like ads

1. **Feedback / testimonial post**: customer photo + their words (with permission)
   + "Salamat po, <name>!" Proof posts are the strongest sellers in PH groups.
2. **Story post**: why you started, a struggle, a milestone ("1st 100 orders!").
   Pinoys support people, not logos.
3. **Helpful post**: "5 bagay na dapat i-check bago bumili ng <product>". Soft CTA
   at the end only if rules allow.
4. **Question / poll**: "Ano mas gusto niyo: spicy or classic?" Builds reach and
   tells you what to make.
5. **Offer post (selling day only)**: clear photo, price, inclusions, area, payment,
   how to order. Price visible. One post, not a flood.

## 5. Partner with admins

- After 2-3 weeks of real participation, message the admin: thank them, offer
  value (a member-only discount code, a giveaway they host, sponsoring a group
  event, free sample for the admin to review).
- Paid "sponsored/pinned post" deals are common in big buy-and-sell groups;
  typical prices vary widely by group size, so ask and test one before committing.
- Any giveaway with prizes + mass reach likely needs a DTI permit (see
  compliance-ph.md). A free sample for honest feedback is not a raffle.

## 6. Build your own group (month 2+)

Once you have 100+ customers, create a group around the customer's interest, not
your brand ("Pasig Lutong Bahay Lovers", "Plantitas of Cavite"). Your page posts the
brand; the group hosts the community, early access, pre-orders and feedback. Invite
customers after every purchase.

## 7. What gets you kicked or ignored

- Same post copy-pasted to 30 groups in an hour (Facebook may also flag it as spam).
- Links in the first line, all-caps, emoji walls, "PM for price".
- Posting on non-selling days, tagging strangers, commenting your link on others'
  posts, "Up!" bumps every hour.
- Fake reviews or dummy accounts praising your shop. Groups find out; trust is gone.

## Cadence for a solo owner (about 5 hours/week)

| Day | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | 15 min helpful comments across 5 groups; reply to all DMs | 1h45/wk |
| Mon | Plan week's posts, collect 2 customer feedback photos | 30 min |
| Wed | 1 value or story post in 2-3 groups (rotate) | 45 min |
| Selling day(s) | 1 offer post per group that allows it | 45 min |
| Fri | Log inquiries/orders per group in the tracker, drop groups that produce nothing for 3 weeks | 30 min |
