# PH marketing calendar

Count forward 90 days from today's date and pull every moment that fits the business.
Fixed-date holidays below are stable; movable ones (Holy Week, Eid, Chinese New Year)
and special non-working days change yearly: WebSearch the current Proclamation if a
date drives a campaign.

## Every month

- **15th and 30th/31st: paydays.** Run offers from payday to 2-3 days after. Pre-tease
  2 days before ("Sahod na sa Friday!").
- **Double-day marketplace sales**: 1.1, 2.2 ... 12.12 on Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop,
  plus mid-month and payday sales on the platforms. 9.9, 10.10, 11.11 and 12.12 are
  the largest.
- **Weekends**: family outings, food orders spike Fri-Sun; B2B content Tue-Thu.

## Month by month

| Month | Moments | Plays |
|---|---|---|
| Jan | New Year, resolutions, Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb), Sinulog (Cebu, 3rd Sun), Ati-Atihan (Aklan) | fitness, planners, budget packs, lucky/red items, Cebu events |
| Feb | Valentine's, Panagbenga (Baguio), end of school-year prep | gifts, couples, flowers, date-night bundles |
| Mar | Summer starts, graduations, Women's Month, Holy Week often late Mar/Apr | grad gifts, summer travel, swimwear, aircon cleaning |
| Apr | Holy Week (lighter ads, reflective tone; many businesses pause), summer peak, Araw ng Kagitingan (Apr 9) | resorts, out-of-town, cooling products |
| May | Flores de Mayo/fiestas, Mother's Day (2nd Sun), Labor Day (May 1) | Mother's Day gifts, fiesta food trays, summer last call |
| Jun | Rainy season starts, back-to-school (timing varies by school system), Independence Day (Jun 12), Father's Day (3rd Sun), Pride Month | school supplies, rain gear, umbrellas, Pinoy-pride campaigns |
| Jul | Typhoons, enrollment, mid-year sales (7.7) | indoor/at-home, delivery, comfort food |
| Aug | Buwan ng Wika, National Heroes Day (last Mon), Ninoy Aquino Day (Aug 21) | Filipino-language campaigns, local pride |
| Sep | "Ber months": Christmas starts in the Philippines; 9.9 sale | early Christmas catalogs, corporate giveaway pitching to companies |
| Oct | 10.10, Halloween, Undas prep, MassKara (Bacolod) | corporate giveaway orders, Undas travel/flowers/food |
| Nov | Undas (Nov 1-2), 11.11 (huge), Black Friday, Bonifacio Day (Nov 30), 13th-month anticipation | gift sets, pre-orders for December, Christmas party packages |
| Dec | 12.12, 13th-month pay (by Dec 24), Simbang Gabi (Dec 16-24), Rizal Day (Dec 30), Christmas, New Year (lucky round fruits, media noche) | Noche Buena bundles, party trays, gifts, year-end sales, OFW balikbayan season |

## Weather

- Rainy/typhoon season roughly Jun-Nov: delivery delays, indoor content, "stay
  home" offers; pause outdoor events or have rain plans. Show empathy, never sell
  hard during a disaster; offer help (donation drives, delivery waivers).
- Hot dry season roughly Mar-May: cooling, travel, outdoor.

## Lead times

- Corporate Christmas giveaways: pitch Aug-Oct; companies finalize by Nov.
- DTI promo permits: apply at least 30 days before a giveaway/raffle starts.
- Marketplace campaign days: register vouchers/campaigns 2-4 weeks ahead in seller
  centers.
- Creator collaborations: book 2-3 weeks ahead; Q4 is booked out.
