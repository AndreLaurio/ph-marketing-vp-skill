# Business archetypes: starting channel mixes

Pick the closest one, state it, then adapt to the brief. "Primary" is where 50%+ of
effort goes for the first 30 days.

## 1. Home-based food / online kitchen (lutong bahay, baked goods, frozen, milk tea)
- **Primary**: local community + foodie Facebook groups, FB page + Messenger.
- **Supporting**: Google Maps (if pickup), GrabFood/foodpanda once demand is steady,
  TikTok process videos.
- **Plays**: pre-order cut-offs ("orders until Thu for Sat delivery"), payday bundles,
  free taste samples to group admins and nearby offices, feedback photos.
- **Watch**: FDA/LGU permits if scaling; delivery radius; food safety claims.

## 2. Online product seller (fashion, beauty, gadgets, home finds)
- **Primary**: TikTok + TikTok Shop or Shopee (choose one marketplace first).
- **Supporting**: FB page + buy-and-sell groups, nano creators with affiliate.
- **Plays**: live selling, bundles, free shipping thresholds, 9.9-12.12 campaigns.
- **Watch**: FDA notification for cosmetics, counterfeit/brand rules, margin after fees.

## 3. Local service (salon, clinic, aircon cleaning, repair, tutoring, pest control)
- **Primary**: Google Business Profile + reviews, local community groups.
- **Supporting**: FB page with booking via Messenger, Google Search ads for high
  intent, referral program.
- **Plays**: before/after posts (with consent), first-visit offer, "areas we serve"
  posts per barangay/city, partnerships with condo admins and HOAs.
- **Watch**: medical/dental advertising limits, DOH/PRC rules for health services.

## 4. Restaurant / cafe (physical)
- **Primary**: Google Maps + city foodie groups + food creators (local micro).
- **Supporting**: Instagram/TikTok, delivery apps, FB page.
- **Plays**: soft opening for creators, signature item to photograph, events
  (acoustic night, watch parties), loyalty card, office lunch deals.

## 5. B2B service / supplier / agency / SaaS
- **Primary**: direct outreach to a built list (lead-scraper) + LinkedIn founder posts.
- **Supporting**: industry FB groups (HR, SME, sellers), chambers, webinars, case
  studies, Google Search for high-intent terms.
- **Plays**: free audit/consult, pilot with 3 logos, referral fee for partners,
  presence at trade events.
- **Watch**: sales cycle is long; KPIs are meetings booked and pipeline, not likes.

## 6. Education / review center / courses / coaching
- **Primary**: FB page + student/parent groups, TikTok short lessons.
- **Supporting**: webinars/free mock exams, alumni testimonials, school partnerships.
- **Plays**: board-exam season calendars, early-bird installment plans, passer
  shout-outs (with consent).
- **Watch**: CHED/TESDA/DepEd claims, guaranteed-pass claims.

## 7. Real estate / property / condo / lot
- **Primary**: FB page + click-to-Messenger ads, OFW groups, Marketplace.
- **Supporting**: property portals (Lamudi, Property24-type sites, Rentpad for
  rentals), YouTube/TikTok tours, broker networks.
- **Plays**: virtual tours, payment computation posts, OFW-hour live Q&A.
- **Watch**: PRC-licensed broker rules, DHSUD license to sell, no misleading ROI.

## 8. Tourism / resort / tours / events venue
- **Primary**: Instagram/TikTok + travel groups ("<Province> Travel Buddies"),
  Google Maps.
- **Supporting**: OTA listings (Agoda, Booking, Klook), travel creators, FB page.
- **Plays**: long-weekend packages, barkada/family bundles, rainy-season promos,
  user-generated content contests (permit if prizes).

## 9. Health, wellness, supplements, beauty clinics
- **Primary**: creator testimonials (compliant), FB page + Messenger, Shopee/Lazada
  Mall-style official store.
- **Watch first**: FDA registration, mandatory "No approved therapeutic claims"
  phrase for food supplements, no cure claims, ad clearance where required.

## 10. Agriculture / farm / provincial products / pasalubong
- **Primary**: town and province groups, OFW groups (gifts), pasalubong centers.
- **Supporting**: Shopee/Lazada for shelf-stable goods, LGU trade fairs, DTI
  Negosyo Center programs and One Town One Product (OTOP) events.
- **Plays**: origin story, harvest videos, corporate giveaway packages in Q4.
