# Copywriting for PH audiences

## Voice rules

- Write like the owner talking to a neighbor, not a brand talking to "consumers".
- Match the audience language: Taglish for mass Luzon, local language for VisMin
  cities, clean English for B2B/ABC1/corporate. When unsure, give one Taglish and
  one English version.
- "Po" for customer-facing replies and group posts to strangers. Not in every line.
- First line is the hook (the feed shows ~2 lines). Price, area and how to order
  must be easy to find.
- 0-3 hashtags, local ones only if they are actually used (#PasigEats). No hashtag walls.
- Real photos > stock. Faces > products. Phone video > polished ad for reach.

## Avoid

- "Elevate", "unlock", "curated", "seamless", "premium quality at affordable price".
- "PM for price" / "PM is the key" (buyers find it annoying; post the price).
- Fake scarcity ("last 2 slots!" every day), fake reviews, unrealistic claims.
- Political or religious takes unless the brand is built on them.
- Jokes about disasters; tone-deaf posts during typhoons or national tragedies.

## Post formulas

**Feedback post**
```
Salamat po, Ate Joy! 🥹 Pang-3rd order na niya this month 💛
"Sobrang sarap ng kare-kare, parang luto ni Lola!" — Joy, Brgy. Kapitolyo

Orders for Saturday delivery open until Thursday 6PM.
Pasig, Mandaluyong, San Juan 📍 GCash / COD
Message lang po kami: m.me/lutongbahaypasig
```

**Story post**
```
2 years ago, natanggal ako sa trabaho at nagluto ako ng adobo para may maibenta.
Ngayon, 1,000 orders na. 🥲

Hindi namin 'to magagawa kung hindi dahil sa inyo, mga ka-Pasig. Maraming salamat.
Para sa 1,000th order celebration: free leche flan sa lahat ng orders this weekend.
```

**Helpful / authority post**
```
Bago kayo magpa-aircon cleaning, i-check niyo muna 'tong 4 na 'to:
1. May chemical wash ba o hugas lang?
2. Kasama ba ang freon check?
3. May warranty ba kung tumulo ulit within 30 days?
4. Legit ba (DTI/BIR, may reviews sa Google)?

Kami po sa CoolFix: oo sa lahat ng 'yan 😊 Quezon City & Marikina.
```

**Offer post (selling day)**
```
PAYDAY BUNDLE 💸 (Nov 14-17 only)
3 ulam + 3 rice = ₱450 (save ₱90)
✔ Good for 3-4 pax
✔ Free delivery within 3km of Kapitolyo
✔ GCash, Maya or COD

Comment "BUNDLE" or message us 👉 m.me/lutongbahaypasig
```

**Question / engagement post**
```
Honest answer lang po: kapag umuulan, ano'ng gusto niyong ulam? 🌧️
A) Sinigang  B) Bulalo  C) Champorado + tuyo
Yung most votes, gagawin naming special sa Friday!
```

**B2B English**
```
Most SME owners in Cebu we talk to lose 6-10 hours a week on manual payroll.
We automated it for 40 companies in 2026. Average setup: 3 days.

If you have 10-200 employees and still use Excel for payroll, I'll do a free
30-minute review of your process. Comment "PAYROLL" or message me.
```

## Messenger saved replies (give the user these, adapted)

- **Greeting**: "Hi po! Salamat sa message 😊 Here's our price list: [image]. Saan
  po ang delivery area niyo para ma-compute namin ang shipping?"
- **Price**: "₱___ po for ___. Kasama na ang ___. Available po GCash, Maya, COD."
- **How to order**: "Para mag-order: 1) Name 2) Address 3) Contact no. 4) Order
  5) Payment (GCash/COD). Confirm namin agad!"
- **Follow-up (24h, once)**: "Hi po! Follow up lang sa inquiry niyo kahapon. May
  tanong pa po ba kayo? Ready na po kami pag ready na kayo 😊"
- **Review request**: "Salamat po sa order! Kung nagustuhan niyo, pwede po ba kayo
  mag-iwan ng review / feedback photo? Malaking tulong po sa small business namin 🙏"
